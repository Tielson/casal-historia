# Paper frames brief — Entre Nós

Precise specs so design can be mirrored in Paper (Figma-like) later. Paper MCP may be unavailable during build; implement against these frames.

## Brand system (landing)

- **Name:** Entre Nós (hero-level signal, not nav-only)
- **Display type:** Cormorant Garamond (or close serif editorial)
- **Body type:** Figtree (geometric humanist sans — not Inter/Roboto/Arial)
- **Palette:** ink `#10181c`, ink-soft `#1c2a31`, mist `#e7eef2`, mist-dim `#c5d2d9`, champagne `#d7c4a3`, copper `#c27a4f`, sea `#3d6b78`, foam `#8fb6bf`
- **Avoid:** purple-on-white gradients; cream+#terracotta broadsheet; flat single-color hero; cards in hero; fake testimonials
